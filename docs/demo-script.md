# Demo script

This is a simulated training walkthrough. Do not present synthetic incidents or AED records as real-world evidence.

1. **0:00–0:30 — Start**: Open the app and choose the simulated cardiac-arrest scenario. Say: “Đây là phiên mô phỏng, không phải ca bệnh thật.”
2. **0:30–1:30 — Triage and guidance**: Confirm the danger check, select the critical triage path, and start the CPR metronome. Say: “Trong tình huống thật, gọi 115 ngay.”
3. **1:30–2:15 — Response**: Trigger the simulated 115 action and view the nearest simulated responder. Explain that ETA is an estimate based on straight-line distance when routing is unavailable.
4. **2:15–3:00 — Dashboard**: Complete the session and open the timeline/dashboard. Show the simulation banner, provenance footer, ADI, and the incident event sequence.

No step claims clinical effectiveness, verified AED availability, or legal validity. The Digital Proof screen must retain its disclaimer: “Căn cứ tham chiếu hỗ trợ hồ sơ theo TT 126/2026/TT-BCA; chưa có giá trị pháp lý cho đến khi cơ quan quản lý Quỹ công nhận.”
